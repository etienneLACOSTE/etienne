Privacy

The site keeps as little as it can. Without an account, nothing about you is stored on the server. Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Etienne Lacoste, who makes and runs this site on his own. To ask anything about your data, write to chessite.etienne@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

  • Your Ches'spell profiles, decks and stats stay in your browser (local storage). They never reach the server, except your profile's name, avatar and deck during an online game, which are shown to your opponent.
  • Online games are kept in the server's memory while they last, then forgotten.
  • Your IP address is used in memory to stop abuse (too many connections or rooms from one address). It is never written down.

With an account

When you create an account, the site keeps:

  • your username, shown to other players;
  • your email, only to send you a password reset link. It is never shown or shared;
  • a one-way hash of your password (scrypt). The password itself is never stored;
  • your sign-ins: for each signed-in browser, when it signed in and when that ends (30 days);
  • if you sign in with Google, the account number Google gives for you. The site does not receive your Google password, email or contacts.

Why: to give you an account that works on every device (the legal basis is the service you ask for). Nothing is sold, shared for advertising or used to profile you.

Cookie

One cookie, sid, keeps you signed in. It is needed for the account to work, so it is set without a consent banner. There are no advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics.

Where it is kept

  • The site runs on Render, in Frankfurt (Germany), behind Cloudflare's network.
  • Accounts are stored in a PostgreSQL database at Neon, in Frankfurt.

How long

Until you delete your account. Expired sign-ins are removed automatically.

Your rights

On your account page you can download everything the site keeps about you and delete your account, which removes all of it at once. You can also ask for a correction, or complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).
