Privacy

The site keeps as little as it can. Without an account, nothing about you is stored on the server. Last updated: 21 November 2026 (Chessdown club on the account, leaderboards).

Who is responsible

Etienne Lacoste, who makes and runs this site on his own. To ask anything about your data, write to chessite.etienne@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

Without an account, you play the campaign against the bot and games on the same screen. Online games need an account (see below).

  • Your Ches'spell profiles, decks and stats stay in your browser (local storage). They never reach the server.
  • Your Chessdown All Star club stays in your browser (local storage): your coach name, the club's name, colours and crest, your players' names, levels and stats. Without an account, it never reaches the server. "Start a new club" deletes it.
  • Your Chessdown career stays in your browser too (local storage): its season, league results, tickets, trophies and the side quests done. Without an account, it never reaches the server. Starting a new club deletes it.
  • Your Chessdown settings (language and timer) stay in your browser (local storage) too.
  • Your IP address is used in memory to stop abuse (too many connections or rooms from one address). It is never written down.

With an account

When you create an account, the site keeps:

  • your username, shown to other players;
  • your email, only to send you a password reset link. It is never shown or shared;
  • a one-way hash of your password (scrypt). The password itself is never stored;
  • your sign-ins: for each signed-in browser, when it signed in and when that ends (30 days);
  • your picture, if you add one, shown to your opponents online;
  • your saved Ches'spell deck (three cards), so that it follows you on every device;
  • your friends: the requests you send and receive, your friends and the players you blocked. Your friends see your username, your picture, and while you have a page of the site open, that you are online and in which game (this is kept in the server's memory only, never written down);
  • your finished games: which game, online, against the bot or on the same screen, the result and how it ended, the opponent's name as shown in the game, how long it lasted and when. It gives your stats and time played on your account page;
  • your Chessdown club and career (the same as in your browser, see above), so that they follow you on every device and so that online All Star games use the club kept with your account. A new club replaces them;
  • your Chessdown leaderboard ratings: for each month and ladder (Classic, All Star), your rating and your ranked series won, lost and drawn; and each ranked series: the two players, the result and when (it limits the series counted between the same two players). Past months are kept for the podium and to start the next season;
  • if you sign in with Google, the account number Google gives for you. The site does not receive your Google password, email or contacts.

Why: to give you an account that works on every device (the legal basis is the service you ask for). Nothing is sold, shared for advertising or used to profile you.

Online games

  • Online games are for players with an account. Your opponent sees your username and picture; in Ches'spell, also your deck.
  • Online games are kept in the server's memory while they last, then forgotten (the result goes to your finished games).
  • In an All Star game, your opponent sees your club's name and, once both have drafted, the players you put on the board with their levels. Your bench stays yours.
  • Leaderboards. Ranked matches change your rating. The leaderboards show your username, rating and ranked results to signed-in players only, never to the public web. On your account page you can hide yourself from them: you keep your rating and still see your own rank.
  • During an online game, the browser tab keeps a random seat key (session storage), so that reloading the page brings you back to your game. It is forgotten when the tab closes.

Chat in online games

Two players who both have an account can chat during an online game. Messages go through the server to your opponent and are not written to any log. The last messages of a game stay in the server's memory while it lasts, so that one can be reported; once the game is over, they are gone. You can turn the chat off at any time; that choice stays in your browser (local storage) only. A player you blocked cannot chat with you, and you can block your opponent from the game.

Reported messages. When you report a message of your opponent's, the site keeps that message, your opponent's account and username, the game, the date and your account, for 90 days, then deletes it. It is kept to deal with abuse (the legal basis is the site's legitimate interest in keeping games safe). Reports are confidential: your opponent is not told that you reported them, and reports about a player are not part of their data download, since in a one-against-one game they would point to you. If they delete their account, the reports about them are deleted; if you delete yours, your reports stay, without your name.

Cookie

One cookie, sid, keeps you signed in. It is needed for the account to work, so it is set without a consent banner. There are no advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics.

Where it is kept

  • The site runs on Render, in Frankfurt (Germany), behind Cloudflare's network.
  • Accounts are stored in a PostgreSQL database at Neon, in Frankfurt.

How long

Until you delete your account. Expired sign-ins are removed automatically, reported chat messages after 90 days.

Your rights

On your account page you can download everything the site keeps about you and delete your account, which removes all of it at once. You can also ask for a correction, or complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).
