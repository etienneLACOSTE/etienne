Chessdown

Chess on an American football field. You build your team before kick-off.

Board
12 rows by 8 columns, end zones at each side
Setup
Draft your pieces with 39 points, one king required
To win
Capture the king, or hold the end zone for a turn
Clock
10 seconds per move
Play
